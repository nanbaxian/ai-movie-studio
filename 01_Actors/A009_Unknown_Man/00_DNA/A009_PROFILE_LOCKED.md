# A009 Profile Locked

The Unknown Man is not a jump-scare character. His fear comes from stillness, direct eye contact, and the fact that the family photo treats him as if he has always been there.

Performance rules:

- He should look calm, formal, and almost too still.
- No monster features, blood, wounds, or supernatural makeup.
- His face should be plausible as a real person in an old family photograph.
- His expression should never become openly angry or theatrical.
- In P002-D, he stands near the center and looks straight into the camera.

Primary EP001 beats:

- Shot 10: Appears in the earliest seven-person family photo.
- Family portrait variants: Only appears in P002-D unless later episodes expand his role.
