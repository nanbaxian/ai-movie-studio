# A005 Profile Locked

Rose Carter is the emotional proof that the family photo anomaly is real. She recognizes the danger before Emily understands it, but fear prevents her from explaining the full truth.

Performance rules:

- Rose should not act like a horror villain.
- Her fear is inward, controlled, and old.
- She avoids eye contact when she knows too much.
- When holding the photo, her hand tension matters more than large facial expression.
- Her line, "Don't look through those photos again," should feel like a warning from memory, not a threat.

Primary EP001 beats:

- Shot 06: Takes the old photo from Emily, freezes, hand begins to tremble.
- Shot 07: Face close-up, controlled terror, gives the warning.
- Family portrait variants: Appears as an ordinary grandmother, unaware in the original photo logic.
