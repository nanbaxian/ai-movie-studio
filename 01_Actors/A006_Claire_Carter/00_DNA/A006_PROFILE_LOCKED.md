# A006 Profile Locked

Claire Carter is the first person erased by the family photo anomaly. Her presence in P002-A must feel natural and emotionally believable, so her absence in P002-B feels like reality has been rewritten.

Performance rules:

- Claire should look warm, ordinary, and slightly protective.
- She should not look mysterious or doomed inside P002-A.
- Her expression in family portraits should be mild and unaware.
- She stands at the far right of the standard six-person family portrait.
- Her outfit must stay consistent across all P002 variants where she appears.

Primary EP001 beats:

- P002-A: present at far right.
- P002-B: erased.
- P002-C/E: remains erased.
