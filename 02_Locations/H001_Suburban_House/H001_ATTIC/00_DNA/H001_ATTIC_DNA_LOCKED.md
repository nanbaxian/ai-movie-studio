# H001-ATTIC DNA LOCKED

Status: LOCATION IDENTITY LOCKED

H001-ATTIC is the Carter family's attic storage space. It should feel like a real suburban attic: cramped, dusty, unfinished, and full of ordinary stored family history. It is suspenseful because something important was hidden there, not because the space is visually monstrous.

Locked visual anchors:

- Low sloped ceiling with exposed rafters
- Unfinished plywood or plank floor
- Dusty cardboard boxes and plastic storage bins
- Cloth-covered objects, old frames, or seasonal storage
- Narrow attic access connected to H001-HALLWAY
- Flashlight beam visible through dust particles
- Small hidden storage area for P001-HiddenPocket or P002-D
- Blue-gray darkness outside the flashlight beam
- No gothic architecture, no basement pipes, no ritual symbols, no overt supernatural props

Spatial rules:

- The attic access direction must match the H001-HALLWAY attic hatch.
- Storage box placement and rafters must stay stable across views.
- P002-D and the back-writing reveal should happen in the flashlight-lit working area.
- Background clutter should support family history but not distract from P001/P002.
