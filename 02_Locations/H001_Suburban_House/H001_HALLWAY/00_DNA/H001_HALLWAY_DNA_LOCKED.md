# H001-HALLWAY DNA LOCKED

Status: LOCATION IDENTITY LOCKED

H001-HALLWAY is the Carter family's ordinary interior hallway. It should feel like a believable middle-class North American suburban home: narrow, familiar, quiet, and slightly tense at night without becoming a stylized haunted corridor.

Locked visual anchors:

- Narrow hallway with warm beige walls and simple white trim
- Ordinary interior doors, including Rose's bedroom door as a repeatable side anchor
- Old framed family photos along one wall, kept indistinct unless a shot specifically calls for detail
- Slightly worn wood floor or muted carpet runner
- Attic pull-down hatch or ceiling access visible near the far end
- Low warm wall sconce, weak ceiling light, or spill light from adjacent rooms
- Far end of the hallway falls into blue-gray shadow during night scenes
- No mansion scale, no ornate hotel hallway, no abandoned-house decay, no overt horror props

Spatial rules:

- The hallway must connect visually to H001-LIVING-DINING, H001-ROSE-BEDROOM, H001-ATTIC, and H001-MIRROR-AREA.
- Door positions, photo frame positions, trim color, and far-end attic access must stay stable across views.
- Background family photos should be readable as family photos but not reveal exact faces unless a production shot requests it.
- Night variants should create suspense through stillness, distance, and shadow rather than exaggerated horror lighting.
